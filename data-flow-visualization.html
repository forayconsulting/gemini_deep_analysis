🧠 AI Agent: Deep LinkedIn Research

Uses Claude Sonnet 4.5 with BrightData MCP for recursive LinkedIn exploration and email generation.

  • Initial Scraping: 5 LinkedIn URLs (partner profile, company, prospect profile, company, jobs page)
  • Recursive Exploration: Up to 5 additional relevant URLs (blog posts, case studies, team pages)
  • Connection Analysis: Finds "needle in haystack" angles:
    • Job postings (team growth, tech stack, strategic initiatives)
    • Shared employers, industries, geographic proximity
    • Overlapping themes in work/interests
  • Email Generation: 3-8 sentence, conversational, highly specific intro request
